# Stakeholder Map — OrbitSleuth

## Key Stakeholders and Interests

| Stakeholder | Interest | Decision-Maker or Influencer | Source |
|-------------|----------|-------------------------------|--------|
| The author (builder) | Demonstrating end-to-end applied-AI and full-stack skill; shipping a complete, evidence-driven, human-controlled system | Decision-maker (sole) | [Q5], [Q6] |
| Prospective hiring managers / recruiters | Evaluating code quality, AI-safety rigor (evidence-linked hypotheses, human control), and polish when reviewing the project | Influencer (their evaluation is the reason the project exists, but they do not direct its scope) | [Q1], [Q2] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

- **Decision-maker**: The author decides scope and priority alone [Q6].
- **Influencers**: Prospective hiring managers/recruiters and general public
  visitors influence what "success" looks like (polish, clarity of AI
  reasoning, evidence discipline) without directing the roadmap themselves
  [Q1], [Q2], [Q3].
